What's included in the true rate

%%WHY%%

Your true hourly rate is gross earnings minus estimated cash expenses and a simplified self-employment tax reserve, divided by total working hours.

It does not estimate federal or state income tax, exact vehicle depreciation, sales tax, or individual tax credits. Use your records and consult a tax professional for filing decisions.
